2. What the Service Is

Repertuvar.app is a repertoire and stage-management tool for musicians. Users create and manage their own repertoire, setlists, lyrics/chords and sheet-music documents for personal and group performance use, and can share and synchronise them with the members of their group or choir. The App also contains curated repertoire metadata (such as title, makam, usul and region) and a limited shared reference catalogue made up only of traditional (anonymous) folk songs and public-domain works. Users can upload their own PDF and musical materials and access them during performances, including offline. The App offers particular support for Turkish music. Features may be updated, added or removed from time to time.
