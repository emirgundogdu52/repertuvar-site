How to reach us

For questions about your account, subscriptions, technical problems, using your setlists, or any other support request about the app, email support@repertuvar.app. To help us solve the issue faster, please tell us which device you use (iPhone, iPad, Android or computer) and attach a screenshot if you can.

For requests about your personal data, your data protection rights or the deletion of your data, please use privacy@repertuvar.app only.

Common topics

  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete My Account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • Subscriptions and payments: In-app purchases are handled by the App Store and Google Play; subscriptions are managed and cancelled from your store account settings.
