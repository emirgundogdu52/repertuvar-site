Refund Policy

Effective date: 08.11.2026 · Last updated: 08.11.2026

This English text is provided for information only. The binding version of this policy is the Turkish one, available at repertuvar.app/refund. In case of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

This policy explains the refund rules for the Repertuvar Pro subscription and for Band/Choir and Edu licences. The service is provided by StageFlow B.V., based in the Netherlands. Which rules apply depends on where you made the purchase.

1. Purchases through the App Store or Google Play

For purchases made in the app (through the App Store or Google Play), Apple or Google takes the payment and handles refund requests under its own rules. We cannot issue refunds for these payments directly.

  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: request a refund from your Google Play order history or the Google Play refund page.

2. Purchases on our website (Paddle)

For purchases on repertuvar.app, our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for these orders; it takes the payment, issues the invoice and handles refunds.

  • 1-month free trial: first-time subscribers get a 1-month free trial on every plan (once per account). You are not charged during the trial; if you cancel before it ends, you are never charged.
  • 14-day no-questions-asked refund: you can request a full refund within 14 days of the first charge after the trial (or of the first payment, for purchases without a trial), without giving a reason.
  • Renewals: later renewals are not refunded. You can cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period and you keep using Pro until then.
  • 1-year one-time payment: this option, offered in some countries (for example to pay with iDEAL | Wero), does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. You can request a full refund within 14 days of the purchase date, without giving a reason; after that, it is not refunded. A refunded purchase is deducted from your Pro time; years you bought earlier are not affected.
  • Band/Choir and Edu licences: for annual per-seat licences, too, you can request a full refund within 14 days of the first payment, without giving a reason; yearly renewals are not refunded. Pro access granted through a refunded licence closes.
  • Charged in error: incorrect charges, such as a charge after cancellation or a duplicate payment, are always refunded.

3. How to request a refund

Use the link in the receipt email Paddle sent you, or look up your order at paddle.net. You can also email support@repertuvar.app with your order email address and we will pass your request to Paddle. Approved refunds go back to the payment method you used; depending on your bank it usually takes 5–10 business days to arrive.

4. After a refund

Pro access closes for the refunded period. Your pieces, setlists and uploaded files are not deleted; they are kept until you delete your account and open just as you left them when you subscribe again (Terms of Use 7.7).

5. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit any statutory rights of withdrawal or refund you have as a consumer that cannot be excluded by contract.

6. Contact

Questions: support@repertuvar.app (Support page)
